Transposition is failing, what should I do?

There may be a few reasons for this happening.

1. Your chord is invalid for the program
    The program only takes one-letter chords, optionally with a '#' after.
    If the first chord is 'Ami' and you want 'Emi', just use 'A'. The 'mi' part will be preserved

2. The song contains invalid chords
    This generally should not happen, but it's more likely to happen with custom songs.
    Check if there are no 'A#'s or other weird chords that have different european equivalents.
    These are the supported bases of chords: A,B,H,C,C#,D,D#,E,F,F#,G,G#
    The first one or two characters of the chord must be one of these.

3. The song format is broken
    Check if all of the chords are properly within []
