How can I edit my HTML songbook (for example font)?

You can edit it using your HTML and CSS knowledge.
See style.css and [songbook].html to understand the structure.

BASIC STRUCTURE
Every song has its id, every song part has its class.
Song part classes are 'verse', 'chorus', 'interlude', 'intro', 'bridge', 'ending' and 'other' (usually tabs)
At the beginning of style.css, you can see the style of basic points in the document. Change them anytime.

EXAMPLES

To change a font, find a font on google fonts, copy the embedded link and add it to <head>
Then in style .css, change the font-family of anything to your desired font (google fonts will also give you the command)

To make the chorus red and bold, do this:
.chorus {
    font-weight: bold;
    color: red;
}
