I can't add a song, what should I do?

These are the steps you should take:

1. Try writing the Song author and Song name with no special characters
    Leave out all commas (,), dots (.) and apostrophes (')
    Don't write 'Bratříčku, zavírej vrátka', instead write 'bratricku zavirej vratka'

2. Type 'idk' instead of the actual song author and look through all of the options.

3. Check akordy-kytary.cz for the song, and check the URL
    If the song isn't there, sorry, it can't be automatically downloaded. See help for manual song addition.
    Some URLs are weird. For example, Bedna od Whisky is stored at akordu-kytary.cz/songs/bena-od-whisky.
        In that case, you have to type 'bena od whisky' (or 'bena-od-whisky') to download that one.
