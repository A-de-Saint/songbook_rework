How does song transposition work?

Song transposition in this program asks you which chord to transpose to and then tries to do the transposition.
Note that transposition focuses on the first chord of the song.

Transposition works in these steps:
1. Find your chord on the transposition circle
2. Find the first chord of the song on the transposition circle
3. Get transposition offset - meaning the number of steps between your desired chord and the first chord
4. Move all of the chords by the offset on the transposition circle

EXAMPLE
Let us have: [Ami][F][Emi][B] in a song and desired transposition chord is [F#]
The transposition circle is A-B-H-C-C#-D-D#-E-F-F#-G-G#
1. F# has the index 9 on the transposition circle
2. A has the index 0 on the transposition circle
3. The offset is (9 - 0) = +9
4. Apply - Ami->F#mi, F->D, Emi->C#mi, B->G
So we get [F#mi][D][C#mi][G]
