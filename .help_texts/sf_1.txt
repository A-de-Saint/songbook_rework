How are songs saved locally?

Songs are saved to the directory song_collection. The song as a text is saved in the songs/ dir
and its 'print' is saved in songlist.txt.

The naming convention of a song text file is name-name_author-author.txt

In every song text file, the first line represents the UTF-8 name of the song,
the second line represents the UTF-8 name of the author.

The next lines represent individual song parts.

Each part starts and ends with \num\, where num represents the type of a song part (see help about adding songs manually).

The actual song lines use the ChordPro format. So there are lyrics and chords are wrapped in [] directly within the lyrics

EXAMPLE FORMAT
Zafúkané
Fleret
\0\
[Ami]Větr sněh [Asus2]zanésl [Ami]z hor do po[Asus2]lí
[Ami]já jdu [C]přes kopce, [G]přes údo[Ami]lí
[C]idu k tvéj [G]dědině zatúla[C]nej
[F]cestičky [C]sněhem sú [E]zafúka[Ami]né[Fmaj7][Ami][Esus4]
\0\
